Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

English Ελληνικά
This Privacy Policy describes how OrthoCalendar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application OrthoCalendar (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password.
  • Contact information: If you use the People feature, we store the names, birthdays, and namedays of contacts you manually enter into the App. This information is stored securely in our database.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: We may collect information about how you use the App, such as the features you use and the time spent in the App.
  • Device information: We may collect information about your device, including device type and operating system version.

Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy, including the use of the Advertising ID on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Create and manage your account
  • Personalize your experience (e.g. language preference, calendar type)
  • Send you relevant notifications about upcoming namedays and birthdays of contacts you have added
  • Display relevant advertisements to free users

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Supabase: Our backend database and authentication provider. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Our advertising partner for free users. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini AI: Our AI assistant feature uses Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Queries sent to the AI assistant may be processed by Google. Google Privacy Policy

We may also share your information if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the OrthoCalendar app. Navigate to Profile → Delete Account.
  • Manual Request: If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you may request deletion by emailing us at koraxfed+ocgeneral@gmail.com. Upon confirmation, we will permanently remove all personal data associated with your account from our active databases, except where retention is required by law.

6. Children's Privacy

OrthoCalendar is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

9. Disclaimer

OrthoCalendar is intended for reference purposes only. While we strive for accuracy, feast days, fasting rules, and saint information may contain errors or vary by local tradition. Always consult your priest or a trusted Orthodox source for canonical guidance.
